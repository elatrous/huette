03 — THE €100 GLOW-UP

Cheap, fast, no rebuild

Start from what you've got

Use an existing counter, or scavenge free pallets / "zu verschenken" timber on Kleinanzeigen for the box. €100 is for the look, not new ply + tile.

Paint it Miami

Roller the front midnight purple, then mask and spray hot-pink + aqua blocks or a sunset fade. One can of each goes a long way.

Hang the donut

An inflatable donut pool float, wrapped or lined with your LED strip, hung over the bar centre on clear wire. Instant centrepiece.

Mount the ladder

Bolt a secondhand chrome pool ladder to one end as pure icon — bottle rack, towel rail, or just the photo moment.

Dress the skirt

Run a batten under the worktop lip and hang things from it: neon tassels, mini swim rings, foam noodles, plastic palms, a fringe curtain.

Light + lock the hatch

LED wash under the top lip and toe-kick; a piano hinge + safety chain + barrel bolt on the lift-up hatch so it stays put.

Keep it fire-safe

  • Cold light only: LED strips run cool — perfect. No candles, halogen or hot bulbs near fabric, foam or the PVC donut (all flammable).
  • Treat the fabric: spray fringe/curtains with a flame-retardant (B1 / DIN 4102 "schwer entflammbar") spray — required for most public venues in Berlin.
  • Power safely: LEDs on a correctly sized PSU and an RCD/FI socket; don't daisy-chain cheap adapters.
  • Clear exits + extinguisher nearby, and keep the dressed skirt off the floor so it can't snag or trail.
04 — SIGNATURE MOVES

The fun bits

🍩

Glowing donut

Inflatable donut float + LED strip, hung over the bar. Reads as a halo / pool float at once — very Miami, very cheap. Swap colours via the strip's remote.

🪜

Iconic pool ladder

A real chrome pool ladder on one end. Chrome catches the neon, screams "pool", and earns its keep as a bottle/towel rack. Secondhand on Kleinanzeigen.

🎏

Dressed skirt

The dead bottom panel becomes the best part: a batten of hung tassels, mini swim-rings, noodles and plastic palms. Cheap, textural, hides the structure.

🌴

Miami colour

Midnight purple base, hot-pink + aqua neon blocks, sunset fade. Done with a few spray cans and your LED — no tiling, no expense.

💡

LED plan

Your strip does triple duty: line the donut, wash the top lip and toe-kick, backlight the menu. One remote to flip pink → aqua → purple.

🪧

Hanging menu

An offcut board, painted, backlit by the strip, hung on wire. Group drinks as lanes/pools; a "Nichtschwimmer" line for the soft stuff.
